About the model

A fast visual approximation

Planetary climate is complex. This simulation approximates six atmospheric convection bands, their Coriolis-driven winds, and the movement of moisture over oceans and terrain.

The formulas are intentionally simple and tuned against Earth. The result is useful for world-building and experimentation, but it is not a fluid-dynamics or weather-forecasting model.

Model notes

Limitations

  • The atmosphere is a single shallow-water layer, without vertical circulation or weather fronts.
  • The ocean uses two coupled layers rather than full 3D bathymetry and does not include tides or sea ice.
  • Temperature is calculated locally and the planet has a fixed spherical radius.
  • Biome mapping extrapolates outside Earth’s observed climate range.
  • Seasonal forcing is represented by solar declination rather than a full orbital model.
  • Point climatology uses equal-length model months; climate zones remain an approximation of observational normals.
  • The empirical parameters are tuned to resemble Earth and are not resolution-independent.
